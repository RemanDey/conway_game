Cellular Automaton Rules
Rule 1: Underpopulation
Any live cell with fewer than two live neighbors dies due to underpopulation.
Rule 2: Survival
Any live cell with two or three live neighbors survives to the next generation.
Rule 3: Overpopulation
Any live cell with more than three live neighbors dies due to overpopulation.
Rule 4: Reproduction
Any dead cell with exactly three live neighbors becomes alive through reproduction.
Interaction Guide: Click or drag on the grid to place cells. Use the control panel to start the simulation, adjust speed, or load predefined patterns. The simulation wraps at edges (toroidal topology).
